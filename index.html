Questions people ask

Does Tidy delete anything permanently?

No. Removing a file in Tidy sends it to the regular macOS Trash, exactly like dragging it there yourself, and every trash action can be undone with one click right after.

Does Tidy need an internet connection?

No, everything runs locally. Tidy only reaches the internet to check for app updates, and never uploads or reads the contents of your files.

Is Tidy really free?

Yes — the full app, with no limits, no account, and no trial period. There's nothing to unlock later.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac on macOS 13 (Ventura) or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.

I see a warning that Tidy is from an unidentified developer.

Tidy is an independent app outside the App Store. The first time you open it, right-click the app icon and choose Open, then confirm in the dialog that appears — you'll only need to do this once.
